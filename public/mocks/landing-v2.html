QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

What’s the time commitment for design partners? ▾
Minimal. We ask for async Slack feedback when you have thoughts, plus one 15-minute call per month. We know your time is valuable — that’s literally why we’re building Enso.
What happens to my email and calendar data? ▾
Your data stays yours. Enso reads your Gmail and Google Calendar through OAuth with read-only permissions. We never store raw email content — we extract relationship signals and discard the rest. We’re building toward SOC 2 compliance.
Do I need Salesforce to use Enso? ▾
No. Enso works entirely from Gmail and Google Calendar. If you use Salesforce, great — Enso complements it by surfacing context your CRM doesn’t capture.
How is this different from Gong or LinkedIn Sales Nav? ▾
Gong records calls but doesn’t help you prepare or follow up. Enso covers the full loop — prep, recording, processing, and follow-ups — all powered by your relationship graph. LinkedIn Sales Nav gives you public profiles. Enso gives you your actual conversation history.
When will I get access after applying? ▾
We review applications within 48 hours. If you’re a fit, we’ll onboard you within a week. Design partners get early access before our public launch and help shape the product roadmap.
What does “free forever” actually mean? ▾
Design partners lock in free access to Enso permanently — no catch, no expiration. When we launch paid plans, your account stays free as a thank-you for helping us build the right product.
