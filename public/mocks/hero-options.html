The reality

Sound familiar?

8:47 AM

Four calls today. You open Salesforce—last note was three months ago, written by someone who’s left the company. You start digging through Gmail instead.

9:30 AM

“Remind me where we left off?” She already told your SDR three weeks ago. It was in a thread you never saw. You scramble.

10:15 AM

Good call with a prospect. You promise to send a follow-up. By the time you open your laptop again, two more meetings have pushed it out of memory.

2:00 PM

Between calls, you try to update Salesforce and write follow-ups. You get through one record before the next meeting starts. The other three won’t get touched today.

6:30 PM

Four meetings done. None felt fully prepped. Half the follow-ups are still drafts. The context was all there—buried across email, calendar, and call notes you never wrote.

“I spend more time prepping, logging, and following up than actually selling.”

— AE at a Series B startup

How it works

The complete meeting loop

Enso handles everything around the meeting so you can focus on what happens in it.

01

Prepare

Before every external meeting, Enso generates a prep brief from your actual email and calendar history. Who you’ve talked to, what was promised, what shifted.

02

Record

Enso captures your meetings through system audio—no bot joins the call. Private, invisible, and works with any platform. Zoom, Meet, Teams, phone calls.

03

Process

After the call, Enso extracts action items, decisions, and commitments. Cross-references them against your relationship graph to surface what actually matters.

04

Follow up

Drafts follow-up emails grounded in what was discussed. Suggests CRM updates. Queues next steps. Nothing falls through the cracks.
